# Incident brief template

## Summary

One paragraph covering impact, duration, and current status.

## Impact

- Affected users or systems
- Severity and measurable scope

## Timeline

Use absolute timestamps and source attribution.

## Contributing factors

Separate confirmed causes, contributing conditions, and open hypotheses.

## Resolution and recovery

Describe the change that restored service and how recovery was verified.

## Follow-up actions

Each item needs an owner, expected outcome, and due date when known.
